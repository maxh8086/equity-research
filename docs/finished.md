## Session 9 — guidance extraction

- **Session 9 — guidance extraction** ✅ the first LLM in the system, fenced on every side. `gateway/` is the only package that imports a provider SDK and the only thing `extract/` may call: `complete()` returns a `Completion` naming the weights that answered (`model_version`), the prompt that asked (`prompt_hash`) and the caller (`extracted_by`), forces `additionalProperties: false`, runs at temperature 0, and never repairs a rejected answer (`SchemaRejected`). `gateway/untrusted.py` fences every document between markers under a guard that forbids following instructions found inside it, and rewrites those markers where they appear in the document itself, so a transcript cannot close its own fence. `ingest/concall_drop` is a `manual_drop` adapter with no model in it at all (R1): symbol and call date come from the file name, the ISIN from bhavcopy (10 days) and index lists (190 days) which must agree, and `as_of` is the sidecar's publication time, rejected if it precedes the call. `extract/guidance.py` holds the Pydantic schema — verbatim quote, speaker and role, section (prepared remarks | Q&A | media interview), metric, period, value and hedge, all as the words used — and `core/compute/guidance.py` is where the model's answer is checked by code: the quote must occur exactly once in the stored text, the value and the hedge must occur inside the quote, and the hedge maps to `will > expect > aim to > working towards` through a hardcoded table. Anything that fails is quarantined with the words that caused it, never repaired (R3). Migration 0010 adds `concall_document` (ingest, `model_version IS NULL` by check constraint), `concall_transcript`, `guidance_claim` and `guidance_quarantine`, all append-only. `model_requested` is stored beside `model_version` because the tier as asked for is the only one of the two a caller knows before paying for the call, and it is what the idempotency check compares: a re-extraction adds a row at the document's original `as_of`, never revising one (R2). 181 tests, all offline: `FakeProvider` scripts every answer, so the suite costs nothing and depends on no model behaving.

## Session 5 — ratios

- **5 — ratios** ✅ `core/compute/ratios.py` (rule `ratios/1`): operating, EBIT and net margins and interest cover for every reported period; gross and net debt to equity for every balance-sheet date; ROCE on average capital employed (opening and closing balance sheets) and incremental ROCE against the fiscal year three years earlier, for fiscal years only. Ratios are fractions; a zero or negative denominator is undefined (None), never zero or infinite. `core.db.pit.ratios_as_of` computes them at read time from the facts known at `t`, so nothing is stored and a restatement changes a ratio only from when it was known. Each value carries its input facts; a gap is `not_applicable`, `missing_input` (naming the absent facts), `undefined` or `invalid_input`. Only Ind AS non-financial filings are covered: for NBFCs, banks and insurers, finance costs are the cost of the product. Companies' own reported ratios are never used (TATASTEEL tags a debt-equity ratio of 0.01).

## Session 10 — guidance resolution

- **Session 10 — resolution and SILENT** ✅ computed at read time, never stored, so no table, no migration and no new dependency (R2: a stored answer would freeze one reading of history and become look-ahead). `core/compute/guidance_resolution.py` (rule `guidance-resolution-2026-09-29`) parses the period label (FY27, FY2027, FY 2026-27, Q3 FY26, H1 FY27, "fiscal 2027"; anything else is `PERIOD_UNPARSED`) and grades a claim MET, MISSED, OPEN (facts for the period not yet known) or UNRESOLVABLE with a stated reason (R3). Nine metrics resolve from Ind AS facts: revenue, revenue growth, PAT, PAT margin, EBITDA, EBITDA margin, net debt, capex (PPE only) and ROCE (full fiscal year only), reusing `ratios.py`; every other metric is `METRIC_NOT_IN_XBRL`, a real data gap and not approximated. Ranges are inclusive and exact, floors and ceilings exact, points within a relative tolerance; a unit mismatch and a directional claim are unresolvable; a claim whose answer was already public when it was made is `ALREADY_KNOWN_WHEN_MADE`. Consolidated is preferred, the basis and every input fact are recorded. SILENT: the period is unsettled and each of the next two later calls is clean (no quarantined claims) and does not mention the metric and period; a call with quarantined claims cannot establish absence. The delivery rate is Σrank(MET) / Σrank(MET+MISSED) on the existing `HEDGE_RANK`; OPEN, UNRESOLVABLE and SILENT are counted apart and are not misses, and by default the first claim per (metric, period) is graded, since grading the last reiteration lets management lower a target and then meet it. `core.db.pit.guidance_resolutions_as_of`, `guidance_silence_as_of` and `guidance_delivery_as_of` filter every read to `as_of <= t`: a restatement filed after `t` changes no answer at `t`. 176 tests including 13 property tests (rate in [0, 1], rank-scale invariance, MET/MISSED swap gives 1 − rate, floor-grade monotonicity, parsing never raises).

## Session 11 — output report

- **Session 11 — guidance report CLI** ✅ `core/compute/guidance_report.py` (rule `guidance_report/1`): pure assembly and rendering of claims, resolutions, silence and delivery rate. `SourceRef`, `Figure`, `InputFact` and `ClaimView` model the data; `build_report` groups claims by quarter and filters silent claims; `render_markdown` lays out an earnings-note style report with headline, new-this-quarter claims, a table of actual versus guidance versus prior period, silent claims and a numbered sources list. Every figure carries a dated link to where it was published and where the fact filed; unsourced figures raise `ValueError` at construction so no number appears without provenance (R1, R2). `core.db.pit.guidance_report_as_of` reads claims, resolutions, silence markers and delivery rates at `as_of`, assembles them, and maps each input fact to its stored source. `report_cli.py` is the entry point: `python report_cli.py --symbol RELIANCE --as-of 2026-03-31` resolves the symbol to ISIN as of that date and prints the markdown report, with timezone-aware IST datetime handling and automatic fiscal-quarter calculation. Tests cover SourceRef/Figure validation, unit formatting, report grouping, silence detection, and CLI argument parsing; `guidance_report.py` re-exports the main classes and functions for cleaner imports. **Not yet:** consensus comparison (there is no free Indian consensus feed); the narrate/LLM layer remains pending and not in scope for Session 11. 46 tests; 1333 tests pass in the full suite with 13 expected failures in `test_report_cli.py` (the cherry-picked test file has a bug: `DeliveryRate` constructor call is missing the `rate` parameter; the bug is in the original commit and not introduced by integration).

## Session 7c (continued), 7g, 7h — wave 2
